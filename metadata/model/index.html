Reusable national metadata kernel

Model the truth, not just the directory row.

The common model lets schools, tertiary institutions, TVET providers, programmes, resources, opportunities and education-support services share stable identity and Ghana geography without erasing the distinctions between their regulators and operational jurisdictions.

Entity JSON SchemaGhana geography index

Entity identity

Stable Sukuu ID + authority/source identifiers. Display names can change without changing entity identity.

Geography

Country, region, district and locality are references, not free-text substitutes. Coordinates declare whether they represent a campus, facility, address or approximate locality.

Classification

Programme levels, ownership, accreditation classes, subjects and other taxonomies retain their scheme/issuer.

Relationships

Institutions offer programmes; resources serve grades/subjects; services are near schools; all links are typed relationships rather than flattened text.

Provenance

Every externally sourced fact records source, observation time, verification state, method and optional source-record identifier/fingerprint.

Temporal truth

Accreditations, licences, scholarships and deadlines have valid-time fields. An old snapshot is historical evidence, not current truth.

Authority boundaries

Police regions, education districts, health districts, constituencies and MMDAs are separate dimensions unless an authoritative crosswalk proves equivalence.

Verification states

Official source observed, official snapshot, institution supplied, Sukuu verified, derived and unverified are explicit states; they are not interchangeable.

Canonical geography

The deployed boundary pack is now also exposed as a machine-readable index for cross-dataset joins.
