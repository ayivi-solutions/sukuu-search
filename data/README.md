# Ghana administrative boundary downloads

These browser-optimized GeoJSON files contain one ADM0 country, 16 ADM1 regions and an integrated 261-district ADM2 layer. The baseline is OCHA/HDX Ghana COD-AB (CC BY-IGO). Guan District is supplemented from OpenStreetMap relation 13804589 (ODbL), removed from Hohoe Municipal and joined to Oti Region. These simplified layers support discovery and visualization; they are not cadastral or legal survey boundaries.
